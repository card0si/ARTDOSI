About Vincent

Raised along the bends of the Ohio River, Vincent Cardosi translates the industrial rhythm of Covington into meditative color fields. His studio practice blends fine art traditions with experimental materials sourced from the neighborhood’s architecture and riverbanks.

Vincent studied painting and sculpture at the Art Academy of Cincinnati and regularly collaborates with regional musicians, dancers, and poets to create immersive environments. His work has been featured in galleries throughout the Midwest and is held in private collections nationwide.

Process & Philosophy

Gathering the City

Vincent collects pigments, textures, and found objects during walks through Covington's alleyways, markets, and riverside parks.

Layered Narratives

Each piece is built across multiple translucent layers, capturing the push and pull between industrial grit and natural flow.

Immersive Atmospheres

Sound, light, and scent often accompany finished works, inviting viewers into a multisensory encounter with place.
